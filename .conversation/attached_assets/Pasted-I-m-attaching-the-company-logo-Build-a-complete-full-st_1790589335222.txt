I'm attaching the company logo. Build a complete full-stack web app (mobile-first PWA) called "Wehda Bus Booking" (وحدة باص) for booking intercity bus seats between Cairo and Alexandria, Egypt. Ticket prices are lower than the station price. The whole UI must be Arabic with full RTL. Currency: EGP.

The app has TWO separated interfaces sharing one backend and database:
- Customer interface at "/" (mobile-first).
- Staff portal at "/staff" (desktop-first, usable on tablet/phone) for employees, supervisors, and admins.

Work in stages, and run and verify the app after each stage:
1) DB schema + seed
2) Trips search + list
3) Seat map with locking
4) Passenger details + pickup step
5) Booking + payment flow + ticket
6) Staff auth, roles, audit log
7) Staff bookings management + "new booking" for phone callers
8) Customers CRM
9) Payments queue + pickup requests
10) Trips board + manifests
11) Reports + admin management
12) Customer account, notifications, support, rating
13) Final design polish

# 1. TECH STACK
- Frontend: React + Vite + Tailwind CSS + React Router. Backend: Node.js + Express. Database: PostgreSQL (Replit built-in) with Drizzle ORM or Prisma.
- Customer auth: phone number + OTP (mock OTP printed in the server console; SMS provider swappable later). Staff auth: email/username + password (bcrypt), session or JWT with expiry, rate-limited. Customers can never access /staff or /api/staff.
- Real-time updates via WebSockets (or polling every 5s) for seat states and staff events. Use Secrets/env vars for all keys.

# 2. CUSTOMER INTERFACE

## 2.1 Home / Search
From (Cairo/Alexandria) with a swap button, To, Date (chips: اليوم / بكرة / اختار تاريخ), Passengers (1-4), button "ابحث عن رحلات".

## 2.2 Trips list
Cards sorted by departure time. Each card shows:
- Departure and arrival time with a line + bus icon and duration between them
- Station names (e.g. "محطة عبود - القاهرة")
- Bus type (VIP/Standard) with amenity icons (AC, WiFi, USB, TV)
- Price with a small green tag "أقل من سعر الموقف"
- Seats left (e.g. "متبقي 7 مقاعد") as a progress bar (green -> orange -> red)
- Booking deadline ("آخر ميعاد للحجز: 30 دقيقة قبل التحرك") with a live countdown
- Status badge: متاح / آخر مقاعد (5 or fewer) / مكتمل / الحجز مقفول
Closed or full trips are greyed out. Filters: time of day, bus type, price range. Sort by price/time.

## 2.3 Seat selection (most important screen)
- Draw the bus as a clean top-down outline: driver seat + steering wheel at the front, door marker, aisle in the middle, 2+2 layout by default (layout comes from the bus record; disabled seats supported). Seats are numbered, large, and tap-friendly.
- Seat states with legend: متاح, مختار, محجوز (not clickable), محجوز مؤقتاً (held by another user, not clickable). Optional admin toggle for ladies-only seats.
- Tap to select/unselect, limited to the passengers count. Seat states update live.
- Seat locking: selected seats are locked for 10 minutes with a visible mm:ss countdown; a background job releases expired locks. Must be race-condition safe: DB transactions + UNIQUE(trip_id, seat_number) for active bookings, so two users can never book the same seat.
- Sticky bottom sheet: selected seats as removable chips, total price, hold timer, "متابعة" button.

## 2.4 Passenger details
One card per passenger with a seat-number badge: full name, phone (Egypt format 01XXXXXXXXX, required for the booker), optional email. Ask for national ID only if the admin enables it in settings.

## 2.5 Pickup point step
The passenger chooses where the bus picks them up, as selectable cards:
1. "من المحطة الرئيسية" (default, no extra cost).
2. "من نقطة على الطريق": predefined admin-managed stops along the route (name, area, time offset from departure, optional extra fee). Examples: مدخل الإسكندرية, الكيلو 21, الطريق الصحراوي, الشيخ زايد, 6 أكتوبر.
3. "من مكان يناسبني" (custom pickup): interactive map (Leaflet + OpenStreetMap, no paid API key) with a draggable pin, "استخدم موقعي الحالي" GPS button, address search, landmark description (e.g. "قدام مسجد كذا"), notes for the driver, and a reachable phone number. Show the note: "طلب النقطة المخصصة بيتراجع من الشركة وهيتم تأكيده قبل الرحلة".
Wait-for-me option (toggle on any pickup type): "عايز السواق يستناني" with a grace period of 5/10/15 minutes and a live-contact phone. Show a disclaimer that the bus waits only up to the chosen time and never beyond the admin's max wait limit.
Pickup statuses: not_required / pending_approval / approved / rejected / suggested_alternative. If a custom pickup is rejected, the passenger is notified and can choose another pickup option or cancel with a full refund.
Price = seat price x seats + pickup fee + waiting fee. Show a clear price breakdown before payment.

## 2.6 Payment
Two selectable cards: Vodafone Cash and InstaPay. Build it as manual verification first, behind a PaymentProvider interface so a real gateway (Paymob/Fawry) can replace it later.
- Show the company's receiving wallet number / InstaPay address (from admin settings) with a copy button, the exact amount in a highlighted box, numbered Arabic instructions, an upload area for the transfer screenshot (with preview), transaction reference, and sender phone.
- Order summary (trip, seats, pickup, total) stays visible.
- After submission the booking becomes "قيد المراجعة" and seats stay held. Staff approves -> "مؤكد" and the ticket is issued; staff rejects -> customer is notified with a reason and seats are released.

## 2.7 Ticket
Designed like a boarding pass: brand header with the logo, a perforated dashed divider with side notches, route with big times, seat numbers, passenger names, pickup point + ETA + Google Maps link, a large QR code, booking reference (e.g. WH-482915), status badge, note to be at the pickup point 15 minutes early. Buttons: تحميل PDF/صورة, مشاركة واتساب, عرض الموقع على الخريطة.

## 2.8 Account and after booking
- Phone + OTP sign-in (guest checkout allowed with phone verification at payment). Profile: name, phone, email, saved passengers, saved pickup locations (home, work).
- My trips: tabs القادمة / السابقة / الملغية with counts. Each booking is a ticket card with a status color strip, live payment status and pickup status, and actions: view ticket (QR), share on WhatsApp, cancel (shows the refund amount first, based on the admin cancellation policy), change pickup point (until a cutoff), contact support.
- Notifications center (bell icon): payment approved/rejected, pickup approved/changed, reminder 2 hours before departure, trip cancellation.
- Support page: WhatsApp button, call button, FAQ accordion, "report a problem" form linked to a booking.
- Rating after a completed trip: 1-5 stars + optional comment.

# 3. STAFF PORTAL (/staff)

## 3.1 Roles and permissions
- Agent (موظف حجز): search/view bookings and customers, create bookings for callers, edit passenger details, add notes, handle pickup requests.
- Supervisor (مشرف): everything an agent can do + approve/reject payments, cancel/refund, override seat locks, view reports.
- Admin (مدير): everything + manage staff accounts, buses, trips, prices, pickup points, settings.
- Audit log: record who did what and when on every booking, payment, cancellation, and edit (shown as a timeline inside each booking). Enforce role-based access middleware on every /api/staff route.

## 3.2 Dashboard
- Cards: رحلات اليوم، حجوزات جديدة، دفعات معلقة، طلبات نقطة ركوب معلقة، مقاعد فاضية، إيراد اليوم, plus a revenue line chart.
- "Needs attention" list: pending payments, pending custom-pickup requests, bookings about to expire, trips departing within 2 hours with low occupancy.
- Always-visible quick search: booking reference, customer phone, customer name, passenger name.

## 3.3 Bookings management
- Table columns: reference, customer, phone, route, trip date/time, seats, pickup type, payment method, payment status, booking status, total, created at, source (app/staff) + staff name.
- Filters: date range, trip, route, status, payment status, payment method, pickup type, source. Quick tabs: الكل، معلقة، مؤكدة، ملغية، مكتملة. Sort, pagination, export CSV/Excel.
- Booking details page (two columns): trip info, seats with passenger names/phones, pickup details with a map, price breakdown, payment proof screenshot with zoom, status timeline. Action panel: approve/reject payment (with reason), approve/reject pickup request (add fee, set ETA, suggest the nearest predefined stop), change seats (opens the trip seat map), move to another trip, cancel with refund calculation, mark as boarded / no-show, resend ticket via WhatsApp link, print ticket. Internal staff-only notes with author and time.
- Bulk actions: approve, export, print manifests.

## 3.4 New booking for a caller (call-center replacement)
"حجز جديد" button: search the customer by phone (create a new record if not found), pick route/date/trip, select seats on the same live seat map, choose the pickup option, record payment (cash at station / Vodafone Cash / InstaPay with reference), and issue the ticket instantly with a WhatsApp share button. Store source = "staff" and the employee's name.

## 3.5 Customers (CRM)
- List: name, phone, total bookings, total spent, last booking, cancellations, no-shows, tags (VIP, تحذير, عميل جديد). Searchable, filterable, exportable.
- Profile: contact info, all bookings, payment history, saved passengers, frequent routes and pickup points, internal notes, tags, activity timeline. Buttons: call, WhatsApp, new booking for this customer, block/unblock.
- Privacy: mask national ID numbers by default; show only with permission, and log every view/export of sensitive data.

## 3.6 Payments queue
Pending Vodafone Cash/InstaPay proofs sorted oldest first, with screenshot, expected vs. entered amount, transaction reference, sender phone, and time waiting. One-click Approve/Reject with reason. Automatically flag duplicate transaction references. Also a pickup requests queue with a map.

## 3.7 Trips board and manifests
- Daily trips board: each trip with an occupancy bar (booked/held/free), departure time, bus, optional driver name, and status. Click a trip for its live seat map (seat -> passenger) and manifest.
- Manifest: passengers ordered by pickup time with seat, name, phone, pickup address, map link, notes, wait-minutes. Print, download PDF, share to the driver on WhatsApp, and mark boarded by checkbox or QR scan with the phone camera.
- Manually open/close a trip, block a seat, or cancel a trip (auto-notifies passengers and starts refunds).

## 3.8 Reports (supervisor/admin)
Revenue by day/week/month, by route, by payment method, by staff member; occupancy per trip; cancellation and no-show rates; top customers; pickup-point usage; ratings. Charts + export.

## 3.9 Admin-only management
Staff accounts (create, disable, reset password, assign role), stations, buses and seat layouts (rows/columns, disabled seats), routes, trips (single or recurring daily schedule with price, bus, and booking cutoff minutes), pickup points per route, and settings: wallet number, InstaPay address, seat-lock duration, cancellation policy, max wait minutes and waiting fee, company contact numbers.

## 3.10 Staff navigation
Right-side navigation (RTL): الرئيسية، الحجوزات، حجز جديد، العملاء، المدفوعات، الرحلات، التقارير، الإعدادات (show only what the role can access). Live badge counters for pending payments and pickup requests. Press "/" to focus the search box.

# 5. BUSINESS RULES
- No booking after the trip cutoff. Max 4 seats per booking. Prices always come from the server, never the client.
- Locked seats expire after 10 minutes if no payment proof is submitted; after proof is submitted they stay held until staff decides (auto-release after 2 hours and notify the customer).
- Server-side validation for everything (phone format, amounts, seat availability). Rate-limit OTP, login, and booking endpoints. Never expose staff endpoints or other customers' data to customers.
- Notifications: in-app + WhatsApp share links for confirmation, rejection, and pickup decisions (structure the code so an SMS/WhatsApp API can be plugged in later). Reminder 2 hours before departure via a background job.
- Real-time WebSocket events for staff: new booking, new payment proof, new pickup request, seat changes.

## 7.2 Colors (design tokens as CSS variables / Tailwind theme)

- Fonts: Cairo 800-900 (or Lalezar for the hero title) for headings, Tajawal for body. Use Western digits (0-9) for prices, times, and seat numbers.
- Sky-blue gradient as the app/hero background with white and orange cards on top, like the logo composition. Add small decorative blocky orange/white squares with black outlines as background accents in the hero and empty states. Footer shows "www.wehdabus.com".
